Hi, I'm Angelica.

Computer Graphics Designer

I am a Computer Graphics Designer student based in Gothenburg, focusing on product and design visualization. Having always been interested in arts and design, I am passionate about creating visually compelling and meaningful experiences.

My work aims to bridge the gap between the surreal and the tangible. I believe that good design should not only be seen but felt. When I'm not rendering, I'm usually cozied up with a good book and a cup of tea or dancing my heart out to my favorite tunes.
